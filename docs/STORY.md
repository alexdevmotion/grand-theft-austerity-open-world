# Story spine

The story is the **spine, not the game**. Free roam, driving, chases, on-foot
action, exploration, side activities and progression must be fun with the
campaign switched off. The campaign gives that world a reason and a shape.

## Premise

George Georgescu — a fictional president fusing the theatrical nationalism of
two real figures — wins power and orders **Builders House** closed, declaring
that independent builders destabilise the nation. His **Ministry of National
De-Acceleration** seals the building. Ilie Bolojan-Agatinei, an exhausted,
relentless reformer-founder, turns that closure into exactly the instability
the regime feared.

All political figures are fictional composites. Călin Georgescu and George
Simion are fused into **George Georgescu**; Ilie Bolojan and Alexandru Agatinei
are fused into **Bolojan-Agatinei**. Nobody is a digital double.

## The four acts

1. **Ordin de Evacuare** — the Ministry seals Builders House. Escape with the
   community server, load it into the Dacia, drive out.
2. **Bootstrap Resistance** — cross the city, rally builders, collect the
   Recorder evidence drive and Nicușor LAN's broadcast credentials.
3. **Term Sheet for the Republic** — reach the broadcast site, replace the
   president's national address using server + evidence + credentials.
4. **Exit Through the Gift Shop** — survive the maximum-heat return chase,
   breach the barricade, enter the Builders House lobby on foot, and liberate
   it. Romanian folk music, dancing builders and restored lights begin only
   after that final interior interaction.

## Political Instability — five Crisis Stars

Stars are the wanted system. They rise from mission beats *and* from free-roam
crime, and each level adds a cumulative response package:

1. Breaking-news strap, radio warning.
2. Inspectors on foot, one checkpoint.
3. Sirens, pursuit vans.
4. Billboard glitches, plaza floodlights, return barriers.
5. City screens flip, crowds erupt, maximum pursuit.

## The Dacia

The **Bootstrapped Dacia 1300** — battered yellow with mismatched purple
panels, taped bumper, sticker decals, dents and rust. Arcade handling:
forgiving at low speed, slow to accelerate, capped top speed, easy handbrake
turns, mild high-speed oversteer, exaggerated visual body roll, one-second
upright recovery. It coughs on first start and the radio comes on with it.

## Builders House

Physically enterable, not a facade. The exterior shell blocks the player and
the Dacia; one pedestrian doorway the car cannot cross. The ground-floor lobby
starts dark and neglected — broken furniture, scattered papers, closure
signage, builders waiting in silence. The same room becomes the afterparty
after liberation.

## Tone

Romanian political satire played straight-faced. Recognisable civic pressure —
barricades, inspectors, broadcast propaganda, austerity signage — turned into
readable game systems. Subtitles stay on for every spoken and radio joke.
