# Visual target

Photographic materials and cinematic open-world composition in a Romanian city
shaped by communist architecture. The September 2026 realism brief supersedes
the original mandatory magenta palette and caricatured proportions. The owned
concept image remains a reference for the cast, yellow/purple Dacia, Builders
House and the satirical premise, rather than a colour-grading target.

- Natural warm sunset against cooler skylight. Skin, concrete and car paint
  retain their identities in daylight, dusk and night. Highlights keep detail.
- Weathered limestone, patched concrete panels, recessed windows, aged asphalt,
  small paving joints and local wetness. Surfaces have different roughness;
  the city should not look uniformly polished or uniformly muddy.
- Occupied rooms vary between warm bulbs and cooler fluorescent fixtures;
  unoccupied windows remain dark. Purple belongs to the builders' identity.
- Cars have believable proportions, cabin depth, restrained glass tint,
  tire sidewalls, functional door hinges and rust concentrated at seams.
- Main characters use real portraits to guide proportions, hair and eyes.
  They remain fictional composites in the existing story. Reference photos
  are studio material, never pasted on the runtime face as lit textures.
- Thin foliage and branching silhouettes replace solid canopy blobs. Wind
  must move the rendered vertices, with bounded geometry and draw-call cost.
- Preserve readable movement, grounded feet, camera control and frame pacing.
  Cinematic presentation must work during play, not only in stills.
